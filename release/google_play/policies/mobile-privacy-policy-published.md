# MOTRI IQ — published app privacy policy

Updated: 25 October 2026. Operator: MOTRI IQ.
Public URL: https://motri-iq.me-0eca.chatgpt.site/privacy/

## About this policy
This Privacy Policy describes how MOTRI IQ handles information in its mobile applications, web application, and related account-deletion request service. The operator is MOTRI IQ. For privacy enquiries, use the in-app Support Center. The Android application identifier is com.motriiq.app.

## Information we handle
Account information includes the name, username, email address, phone number and authentication information you provide or use to sign in. Listing and profile information includes vehicle or marketplace descriptions, prices, contact details, business information, photos and location details you choose to provide. We also store in-app messages and offers, favorites, saved searches, alerts, follows, reviews, reports and related activity records when you use those features. Contacting support or requesting deletion provides the account identifier, reply contact and information you include in your request.

## How information is used
Information is used to create and manage accounts; publish and display listings and profiles; provide search, comparison, favorites, messaging and other requested features; connect users with sellers; respond to support and deletion requests; and review reports, protect accounts and prevent misuse. Public listing and profile information is visible to other users. Do not put passwords, login codes or information you want to keep private into public listings or messages to unknown people.

## Photos, camera and location
You choose photos through system photo pickers or the camera when using a feature that needs an image. Selected images may be uploaded to Supabase storage for the listing, profile or relevant feature. Business-location selection can request foreground device location permission. MOTRI IQ does not run background location tracking. You can deny or revoke camera or location permissions in device settings; the related feature may then be unavailable. The app does not need access to your contacts, SMS inbox or call history to browse listings.

## Service providers and external services
Supabase provides account authentication, database and uploaded-file storage. The account-deletion website uses Sites hosting and a database for request details, status and timestamps; its administrator queue requires authorized access. Hosting and network providers receive connection information needed to serve requests, such as IP addresses and browser/request information. When you choose calling, WhatsApp, maps, external links or sharing, the selected information is passed to the external service and its privacy policy applies. Public listings may be copied or saved by people who can view them. We do not sell your personal information or listing data.

## Advertising and device information
The first Google Play release, version 1.0.1 with version code 5, does not include Google Mobile Ads or Google UMP. AdMob banners and ad-privacy controls are disabled, advertising-ID permissions are removed, and paid business-ad feeds are suppressed in this Play build. This release does not request advertising identifiers through an ad SDK. Earlier preview builds included Google Mobile Ads and requested non-personalized advertisements; Google SDKs in those older builds may process network addresses, device identifiers, app interactions and diagnostics. The web application does not initialize the Android mobile-ad banner. Ordinary user-posted vehicle and marketplace listings remain app content.

## Local storage and platform differences
The app stores settings such as language, appearance and notification preferences locally. Web sign-in uses browser storage for the authentication session. Clearing browser data, signing out or uninstalling can remove local information, but does not itself delete your backend account or public listings. A local notification preference does not itself subscribe you to an email or push-delivery service. Paid business placements and wallet top-ups are disabled in the first Google Play release. Existing wallet or receipt records from other or earlier versions may remain in the backend and are included in relevant access or deletion enquiries.

## Data retention
Account, listing, uploaded-file, message and feature records are stored in the backend to provide the service and are not automatically erased when you close or uninstall the app. Local preferences remain until changed or cleared on your device. Deletion-request information is stored to verify ownership, follow up and record the request status. Retention and removal depend on the type of record and the account/deletion process; a single fixed retention or automatic-expiry period has not been established for all categories. Ask support for the records held for your account, the applicable retention period and any security, fraud-prevention or legal reason for retaining particular records. Backup removal also depends on the service-provider configuration and is not immediate merely because a form was submitted.

## Request account and data deletion
You can request deletion of your MOTRI IQ account and associated data in Settings → Delete account and data or through the [public deletion-request website](https://motri-iq-account-deletion.me-0eca.chatgpt.site/). You do not need to install the app or sign in to the request website. Provide your account identifier and a contact method; do not send your password or one-time login codes. Requests are reviewed manually, and support verifies account ownership before processing. Submitting the form records a request; it does not automatically delete the account, listings, files or messages. Support should confirm the processing scope, expected completion time and any retained records with you. A fixed completion deadline is not currently published. Contact the in-app Support Center for the status of a pending request.

## Security and your choices
The app connects to hosted services over HTTPS and uses authenticated sessions and backend access controls. Account-deletion administration requires authorized sign-in. No internet service can guarantee absolute security. Protect your account credentials, review what you publish, and report suspected misuse to support. You may ask support for access to, correction of, or deletion of your account data and for information about service-provider processing locations. Do not upload another person’s private information without authority.

## Changes and contact
Updates to this policy will be published on this page with a revised date. This page covers MOTRI IQ itself; external services have their own policies. For questions about this policy, data practices, retained records or a deletion request, contact MOTRI IQ through the in-app Support Center.
