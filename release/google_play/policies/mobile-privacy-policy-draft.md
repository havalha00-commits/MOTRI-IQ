# MOTRI IQ — mobile privacy policy draft for publisher review

Updated: 24 October 2026. Contact: in-app Support Center.

Publisher must confirm its legal identity, retention periods, processing location, service-provider contracts and actual moderation/deletion practices before hosting this as the store policy. Do not use the portal-only privacy notice as the mobile app policy URL.

MOTRI IQ uses account names, usernames, email/phone identifiers and authentication information to provide accounts and seller features. Users choose listing descriptions, contact details, photos, business information and optional location details; public listing content is visible to others. In-app offers and messages are stored on Supabase and displayed to their participants. Favorites, saved searches, alerts, reviews, reports and activity records support app functionality and moderation.

Supabase supplies authentication, database and file storage. Google Mobile Ads and UMP supply Android ads and privacy choices; Google SDKs may process IP addresses, advertising/device identifiers, app interactions and diagnostics. Non-personalized ad requests do not mean no data is collected. Calling, WhatsApp, map and share actions hand chosen information to external apps/services whose policies apply.

Photos are selected using system pickers or the camera. Business location selection requests foreground location permission; the app does not run background location tracking. Device preferences and notification settings are stored locally. A local notification preference is not evidence of a push-delivery service.

Paid business placements and wallet top-ups are disabled in this Google Play build. Legacy backend wallet/receipt data and authorized staff functions still exist and need proper access and retention controls.

Account deletion can be requested in Settings → Delete account and data or at https://motri-iq-account-deletion.me-0eca.chatgpt.site/ without installing or signing in to the app. Requests require ownership verification. Request recording does not itself perform backend deletion. The operator must define the deletion scope, exact processing time and any legally justified retained records/periods before publishing this policy.

Confirm whether any data is sold, used for personalized advertising, shared beyond listed providers or retained in backups. The final policy must accurately disclose those practices. Contact support for access/correction/deletion enquiries and never send passwords or one-time login codes.
