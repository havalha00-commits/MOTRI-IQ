Discover cars and local listings with MOTRI IQ.

Browse vehicle listings, compare key details and use search filters to narrow your options. Explore the marketplace and discover local businesses in one app.

• Browse car photos, prices and listing details.
• Search and filter vehicles to find relevant options.
• Save favorites and revisit searches with your account.
• Compare cars and contact sellers using the options on their listings.
• Explore marketplace listings and local business information.
• Use Arabic, Kurdish or English, with light and dark appearance options.

MOTRI IQ connects buyers and sellers. Listing information is supplied by users. Check the vehicle, documents, seller identity and transaction terms independently before making a purchase. MOTRI IQ is not a vehicle inspection service or a government service.

Paid business placements and wallet top-ups are unavailable in this Google Play version.
